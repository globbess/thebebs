    Getting there

    Rösås is near Bäckaby, deep in the countryside, about half an hour south of Vetlanda. It’s wonderfully in the middle of nowhere, so on the wedding day we’ll arrange transportation from Vetlanda. Details to come.

    International
    By air
    Fly into Copenhagen, usually the easiest, or Stockholm.
    By train
    From Copenhagen: A direct train from the airport reaches Växjö in about 2½ hours, and from there it’s about 50 minutes by car. You can also ride all the way to Vetlanda with a change in Nässjö, but the last leg only runs a few times a day.From Stockholm: Direct trains from Stockholm Central to Nässjö run about every hour and take around 3 hours. From there, hop on the local Krösatåg to Vetlanda (about 30 minutes), then it’s about half an hour by car. Landing at Arlanda? Take the Arlanda Express into Stockholm Central first.Download the SJ app to check times and buy tickets for every leg, including the local trains.
    By car
    About 3½ hours from Copenhagen Airport, or 4½ from Stockholm.
    Locals
    By air
    The closest airports are Växjö, about 50 minutes away, and Jönköping, about 1 hour 20.
    By train
    From Stockholm, take the train to Nässjö, then the local Krösatåg to Vetlanda, about 30 minutes.
    By car
    About 3 hours from Gothenburg or Malmö, and 4½ from Stockholm.
